## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

* **Zero-Lag Precision Touchpad**: 1-finger glide, tap left-click, 2-finger right-click, 2-finger scroll, and 2-finger pinch-to-zoom (`Ctrl + Wheel`).
* **🎯 Gyroscope Air-Mouse**: Steer your PC cursor by tilting your phone in the air. Filtered with jitter dampening for smooth navigation and presentations.
* **🛋️ Living Room Couch Suite**:
  * 15m, 30m, 45m, 60m sleep timers with a live countdown display on your phone.
  * Power actions: Sleep Now, Lock Workstation (`Win+L`), Restart, Shutdown.
  * **⚡ Wake-on-LAN**: Broadcasts physical MAC address; wake sleeping PCs with a single tap.
* **💻 PC-to-PC Controller**: Use a Windows/Linux laptop or Surface tablet as a giant wireless trackpad for your main desktop or TV gaming PC!
* **⌨️ Summon Keyboard**: Real-time Unicode text streaming directly into any search bar or text field.
* **🦊 Firefox Quick-Launch Dock**: One-tap website shortcuts that trigger Firefox on your PC.
* **🔇 Silent System Tray Host**: No console windows, runs quietly with the Husky mascot icon.
* **🛡️ TAB inc. Privacy Guarantee**: 100% local Wi-Fi communication, zero tracking, zero telemetry, no accounts required.
