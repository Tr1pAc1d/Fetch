Built-in Guide
🖥️ Multiple PC Profiles
Tap the top PC pill anytime to switch between multiple computers (e.g. your desktop, laptop, or living room PC) with 1 tap.
👆 1-Finger Touchpad
Glide on the surface to move your mouse cursor. A quick tap triggers a Left Click.
✌️ 2-Finger Scrolling & Right Click
Drag two fingers up or down to scroll web pages and documents. Tap with two fingers for a Right Click.
⌨️ Search Bar Live Typing
Click any search bar or text box on your PC, then tap the keyboard icon at the bottom. Your typing streams live to your PC cursor. Press Enter to submit!
🌐 Quick Links (Firefox)
Tap the globe icon in the top header to launch YouTube, Reddit, or your favorite saved sites directly in Firefox on your computer.
